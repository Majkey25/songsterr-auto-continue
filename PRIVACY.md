# Privacy and use of Songsterr Auto Continue

Updated 5 October 2026. Developer: Matěj Teplý, publishing as Majkey / Majkey25. Contact: [majkeylab@gmail.com](mailto:majkeylab@gmail.com).

## Data processed on your device

The extension runs only on `https://www.songsterr.com/*`. It inspects visible dialog text, links, and controls in the page to find Songsterr's free “continue with sync pauses” action and click it. This inspection happens inside your browser. Page content, account details, browsing history, and audio are not sent to the developer.

There is no account, persistent storage, cookie, analytics, telemetry, advertising SDK, background network service, or remote executable code. Temporary references to page elements exist only while the content script is running. The extension uses page information only to activate the disclosed free continuation action, never for advertising, sale, or profiling.

## Control and deletion

Disable or remove the extension from the browser's extensions page and reload open Songsterr tabs to stop its content script. Closing the page releases its temporary state. There is no developer-held copy of extension data to delete. Your Songsterr account, cookies, playback, and purchases remain governed by Songsterr; this extension does not manage or delete them.

## Support and external services

Songsterr receives normal visits and the continuation action through its own website. Opening this repository or its issues contacts GitHub under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), including normal connection information such as your IP address. Email support receives the address and content you choose to send. Do not post private account information or page HTML publicly. Contact the email above about access to or deletion of support messages, which are kept only as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations.
